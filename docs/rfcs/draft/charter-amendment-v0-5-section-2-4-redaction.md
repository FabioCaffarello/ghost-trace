## Motivation

§2.4 is the third object-level Charter invariant redacted in committee mode (§2.5 was first v0.3 per [`§0013`](../../charter/decision-log.md); §2.3 was second v0.4 per [`§0017`](../../charter/decision-log.md)). Decision-log [`§0008`](../../charter/decision-log.md) redaction order places §2.4 after §2.3. [`§0022`](../../charter/decision-log.md) implementation pivot established the empirical-pressure-phase posture: redaction resumes when implementation surfaces concrete questions the Charter does not already answer. [`§0077`](../../charter/decision-log.md) empirical-pressure assessment catalogued six concrete Q-§2.4-* questions surfaced by implementation work (wire shape, reference target, triggering condition, acyclicity, Q5-transitive, §2.3 BC1 inheritance), identifying §2.4 empirical pressure as strong — sufficient material for redaction resumption.

Without §2.4 frozen, the post-OMQ #2-C + OMQ #3-α substrate commitments are present in [`provenance-model.md` §Inferential Provenance](../../ontology/provenance-model.md) but lack a constitutional anchor connecting them into a single inferential-influence invariant. §2.4 supplies that anchor: every Assertion under inferential influence declares it structurally via typed `subject_ref_*` edges read as `influenced_by` chains, committed at substrate-time per OMQ #3-α and read against the referenced hypothesis's §2.5 lifecycle event chain per OMQ #2-C. This codifies the central [§1 Thesis](../../charter/constitutional-charter.md#1-thesis) defense against recursive belief inflation (per §2.4 stub L150 "the invariant that most directly defends against the central failure mode").

## Alternatives Considered

- **Path Reading 2 — narrow redaction.** Rejected per Q1 ratification (Round 3). Three grounds: (i) five-item merge checklist Item 2 requires section structure parallel to §2.1/§2.2 — narrow breaks parallelism (mechanical checklist failure); (ii) [`§0077`](../../charter/decision-log.md) catalogued 6 concrete questions ancored in §2.4 — narrow leaves 3-4 unaddressed, migrating weight to §2.6 redaction and discarding §0077 function; (iii) Steps 1.1–1.4 conducted assuming Reading 1 (three forward-reference markers, AP3 candidate, BC1-BC7 mapped, vocabulary path decisions) — narrow discards structural work without proportional gain.
- **Variant β — AP3 split into two separate APs (substrate-side + prose-side).** Rejected per Q5(a') ratification (Round 9). Failure-mode-root is the same (missing influence declaration, manifesting in substrate OR in prose); split fragments root without distinct-root justification per §7 minimalism. No frozen §2.x AP precedent uses share-root split.
- **Variant γ — AP3 hierarchical (prose-side primary; substrate-side as detection wing).** Rejected per Q5(a') ratification. Hierarchy explicit risks judgmental tone; no §2.x frozen AP uses hierarchy; departs from §2.3 AP1 compact "X or Y" precedent. Variant α (monolithic with detection-sentence asymmetry signaling §2.4-originality via committee-mode review novelty) preserves precedent + Coherence finding 4 fidelity simultaneously.
- **BC8 (§2.4 vs §2.6 boundary preview) inclusion.** Deferred per Q6 ratification (Round 6). Three grounds: (i) §2.3 BC1 preview was load-bearing because of shared edge structure (high confusion risk); §2.4 vs §2.6 have distinct structures (edges vs fields) — preview less load-bearing; (ii) Marker 3 Piece 1 SR clause already carries cross-reference §2.4 → §2.6 functionally; (iii) [`§0077`](../../charter/decision-log.md) Q-§2.6-1 wire-shape degrees of freedom create amendment-risk if §2.4 BC8 commits boundary before §2.6 chose wire shape. §2.6 redaction will codify mutual scope from §2.6 side at its Gate (mirror of §2.3 BC1 + §2.4 BC1 mutual-scope completion pattern).

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Future RFCs revisiting OMQ #2-C ([`§0020`](../../charter/decision-log.md)) or OMQ #3-α ([`§0021`](../../charter/decision-log.md)) resolutions without new evidence — those resolutions are now anchored by §2.4 binding text and inheritance via these RFCs is the load-bearing structural commitment.
- Premature Layer B substantive specification. Marker 3 Piece 2 BC5 + §2.5 BC3 codify that Layer B's deep criterion form is deferred to the follow-on RFC pending §2.6 redaction; opening Layer B RFC content before §2.6 closes would violate the §0011 deferral contract.
- Treating Marker 3 Piece 1 activation as discharge. Per §0099 methodological observation 2 tripartite distinction: activation makes the declared-influence half testable; discharge requires Layer B RFC substantive content drafted post-§2.6. Conflation prematurely closes Layer B RFC's on-hold status.
- Silent generalization of multi-category-inferential-traversal scope. BC7 codifies that the structural commitment is §2.4 territory; runtime traversal mechanics (graph indexes, query layers, projection-rebuild paths) are below §2.4. Architecture documents must respect this scope.
