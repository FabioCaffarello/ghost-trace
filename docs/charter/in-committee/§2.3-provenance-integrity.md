# §2.3 Provenance Integrity — committee draft

**Status:** in-committee draft. Not binding. Do not cite as authoritative.

## Anchor

> **Status:** Pending committee redaction.
>
> **Working definition (non-binding):** Every assertion declares, in its structure, the observations and prior assertions that constitute its provenance. The capacity to reconstruct the path from any assertion back to underlying observations is guaranteed by the immutability properties of the primary event log.
>
> **Anti-pattern this invariant will forbid:** orphan assertions; aggregations that destroy granular traceability; caches that replace rather than supplement the provenance path.
>
> **Distinct from Inferential Influence Disclosure:** this invariant addresses *observational* provenance — where the assertion came from. The next invariant addresses *inferential* provenance — which prior beliefs influenced its formation.
>
> — [Charter §2.3](../constitutional-charter.md#23-provenance-integrity)

## Forbidden Anti-Patterns

**Inventory elements mapped here:**

- A3 — Orphan assertions (refined under Q3-B: assertion where the populated `subject_ref_X` does not resolve to an existing record, OR no `subject_ref_X` populated — violating oneOf exclusivity per §0016).
- A4 — Aggregations that destroy granular traceability (refined under §0010/§0011/§0016 + Reforço 1: aggregations are Cat II constructs; aggregation that fails to record its derivation inputs — the Cat I primaries it derived from — loses the chain. Cat II constructs are transit, not termini; the granularity-destroying anti-pattern is the failure to preserve the chain through the Cat II transit point).
- A5 — Caches that replace rather than supplement the provenance path (refined under §2.1 Boundary Conditions: projections may not replace substrate; §2.3 specializes the §2.1 anti-pattern for provenance specifically — projection-replacing-substrate-provenance is the specific failure mode).

## Boundary Conditions

**Inventory elements mapped here:**

- A6 — §2.3 vs §2.4 scope boundary (§2.3 addresses observational provenance; §2.4 addresses inferential influence).
- B1 — Category II constructs are chain transit, not chain termini (per Reforço 1). The reconstructibility guarantee terminates at Cat I primaries.
- B3 — Hypothesis content is not §2.3 scope (per §2.5 BC5: hypothesis-internal structural commitments — provenance of its own evidence chain, confidence, evidential independence, declared influence — are §2.3/§2.4/§2.6 territory respectively; §2.5 governs lifecycle events about the hypothesis). §2.3 governs the chain *to* the lifecycle events; the hypothesis's own evidence-chain provenance is its own §2.3 subject. Recursion bottoms out at Cat I primaries.
- B6 — Identity-tier resolution is not §2.3 scope. Identity-tier consistency default per §0015 carries through provenance chains; formal Identity-tier resolution is forward-referenceable per §0011 precedent and §0014 empirical assessment verdict.
- B2 — Subject_ref field-naming literals are not §2.3 scope. The §2.3 binding text encodes the structural mechanism (typed reference fields per Q3 resolution); the specific field-name literals (`subject_ref_observation`, etc.) live in `entity-model.md` Assertion section, not in Charter binding text. Per Reforço 2; final decision at Step 1.5.
