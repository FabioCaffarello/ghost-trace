# §2.3 Provenance Integrity — committee draft

**Status:** in-committee draft. Not binding. Do not cite as authoritative.

## Anchor

> **Status:** Pending committee redaction.
>
> **Working definition (non-binding):** Every assertion declares, in its structure, the observations and prior assertions that constitute its provenance. The capacity to reconstruct the path from any assertion back to underlying observations is guaranteed by the immutability properties of the primary event log.
>
> **Anti-pattern this invariant will forbid:** orphan assertions; aggregations that destroy granular traceability; caches that replace rather than supplement the provenance path.
>
> **Distinct from Inferential Influence Disclosure:** this invariant addresses *observational* provenance — where the assertion came from. The next invariant addresses *inferential* provenance — which prior beliefs influenced its formation.
>
> — [Charter §2.3](../constitutional-charter.md#23-provenance-integrity)

- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party reads Assertion records from substrate, inspects `subject_ref_*` field populations, performs transitive resolution to terminal Cat I records. No cooperation from the asserting service required.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Declares structurally" → typed reference field on the Assertion entity per §0016. "Observations" → Category I primary observations per §0015 + [`entity-model.md` Cat I](../../ontology/entity-model.md). Caveat: stub's "and prior assertions" co-occurring clause overlaps A6 scope tension (§2.4 territory). Vocabulary/scope carry-forward to Step 1.4: binding text must excise "and prior assertions" or scope-restrict.

**Overall: Pass with caveat (scope: stub "and prior assertions" excision).**
