# §2.5 Hypothesis Lifecycle Explicitness — committee draft

**Status:** in-committee draft. Not binding. Do not cite as authoritative.

## Anchor

> **Status:** Pending committee redaction.
>
> **Working definition (non-binding):** Operations on hypotheses — formation, merge, split, dissolution, promotion, demotion — are recorded as immutable events in the primary event log. The current state of any hypothesis is a projection over the history of operations applied to it, never the result of direct mutation.
>
> **Anti-pattern this invariant will forbid:** direct mutation of hypothesis state in graph or document stores; loss of evolution history; cluster merges without recorded antecedents.
>
> — [Charter §2.5](../constitutional-charter.md#25-hypothesis-lifecycle-explicitness)

## Committee notes

### Step 1.2 — Falsifiability findings

The four-question test of [`falsifiability-check` §1](../../../.claude/skills/epistemic/falsifiability-check/SKILL.md) was applied to the eight grouped elements identified in Step 1.1, producing 32 cells. One element (B(Layer-B-deferred)) was handled separately as a methodological forward-reference question; the two-reading deliberation appears below.

#### Per-element falsifiability findings

##### A1 — Operations enumeration

> Claim: the six operations on hypotheses are `formation`, `merge`, `split`, `dissolution`, `promotion`, `demotion`.

- **1.1 Violation — Pass.** Falsifying state: a lifecycle event exists whose `operation_type` is not in the enumerated six. Detectable by type-system enforcement of the enum.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party reads operation events from substrate, counts distinct `operation_type` values, compares against the enumerated set. Mechanical.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Six operations" → enumerated enum values. "On hypotheses" → restricted to Category III records. Each term reduces to type-system structures or to operations on them.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass.** `Hypothesis`, `promotion`, `demotion` glossary-canonical. `formation`, `merge`, `split`, `dissolution` are non-circular transparent operational nouns (glossary-absent — carry-forward to Step 1.4).

**Overall: Pass.**

##### A2 — Recording requirement

> Claim: operations on hypotheses are recorded as immutable events in the primary event log.

- **1.1 Violation — Pass.** Falsifying state (two faces): a hypothesis whose current state implies an operation with no substrate event; or an operation event whose content-addressable identifier recomputed on read fails to match. (b) is §2.1's inherited mechanism.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party recomputes identifiers and cross-references projection vs. substrate replay. No cooperation.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Recorded" → committed to primary event log. "Immutable" → §2.1 substrate guarantee. "Primary event log" → canonical-whitelisted phrase.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass with caveat.** All operative terms reduce to existing definitions. Caveat: stub uses bare `events`; [glossary `observation` forbidden synonyms](../../glossary.md) prefer `lifecycle event` for hypothesis transitions. Flag vocabulary preference for binding text.

**Overall: Pass with caveat (vocabulary: `events` → `lifecycle events`).**

##### A3 — State-as-projection

> Claim: the current state of a hypothesis is a projection over the history of operations applied to it, never the outcome-of-mutation form.

- **1.1 Violation — Pass.** Falsifying state: a projection reports a hypothesis value that cannot be reproduced by replaying operation events from formation onward. Concrete artifact: discrepancy between substrate-replay-output and projection-query-output at a given timestamp.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party runs replay against substrate, queries projection, compares. Asymmetry is the violation; comparison is mechanical.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Current state" → projection's materialized value at query time. "Projection over history" → fold over operation events. "Direct mutation" → projection write with no corresponding substrate event.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass with caveat.** `projection` and `hypothesis` glossary-canonical. `operations` defined in A1. Caveat: `state` is on ambiguity-reducer watchlist (13 terms post-§0008); not formally defined as constitutional term. Pass non-circularity by reference; surface for binding-text local operationalization or accept watchlist advisory.

**Overall: Pass with caveat (vocabulary: `state` watchlist advisory).**

##### A4 — Anti-patterns (grouped: A4.a, A4.b, A4.c)

All three share the same overall verdict (Pass with caveat). Caveat-sources differ per anti-pattern; treatment is grouped with per-anti-pattern caveat notes.

> A4.a: direct mutation of hypothesis state in graph or document stores.
> A4.b: loss of evolution history.
> A4.c: bare-term merge phrasing — recorded antecedents required.

- **1.1 Violation — Pass (all three).** A4.a: projection write without corresponding substrate event. A4.b: hypothesis present in projections whose substrate operation-event chain is unretrievable. A4.c: a merge event with empty or non-resolving `antecedent_ids` field.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass (all three).** Each falsifying state above detectable from substrate without cooperation.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass with caveats (vocabulary, not falsifiability).** A4.a operationalization is concrete; vocabulary caveat: "graph or document stores" names backends — binding text reformulates as substrate-vs-projection-role naming. A4.b operationalization is concrete; minor caveat: `evolution history` is operational but `operation history` is more precise. A4.c operationalization is concrete (antecedent field presence); vocabulary caveat per Step 1.1: bare-term merge phrasing is post-§0010 stale — canonical is `Hypothesis-merge` (abstract) or per-subtype name.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass (all three).** "Direct mutation" defined contrastively with projection-over-history (A3). "Evolution" reduces to operation chain (A1/A2). "Antecedents" reduces to prior Hypothesis records the merge derived from.

**Overall A4: Pass with caveats (three vocabulary corrections; all Step 1.4 work, no 1.3 fails).**

##### B1 — Type structure

> Claim from §0010 Candidate A.2: abstract `Hypothesis` type with four sibling concrete subtypes (`BehavioralCluster`, `CoordinationRing`, `CampaignHypothesis`, `AutomationGroup`).

- **1.1 Violation — Pass.** Falsifying states: a record whose declared type is the abstract `Hypothesis` directly (no concrete sibling); or a record whose type is a fifth subtype outside the enumerated four. Both detectable by type-system enforcement.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party inspects declared types, cross-references the enumerated four, verifies abstract type has no direct instances. Mechanical.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Abstract type" → a type without direct instances (concrete technology deferred per §0003; structural property is decidable independent of technology). "Concrete subtypes" → the four enumerated record types.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass.** All five names glossary-canonical post-§0010.

**Overall: Pass.**

##### B(Layer-A) — Cadence gate

> Claim from §0011: a promoted hypothesis satisfies Layer A when elapsed time since the promotion event exceeds a parameter `N` recorded on the promotion event or on the hypothesis's concrete subtype.

- **1.1 Violation — Pass.** Falsifying state: hypothesis marked Layer-A-positive while `T_now − T_promotion < N`. Detectable structurally: substrate records `T_promotion`; `N` is a field on the promotion event payload; `T_now` is observation time.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass.** Third party reads promotion event timestamp, reads cadence parameter, computes elapsed time, compares against Layer A outcome state. Fully mechanical.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass.** "Elapsed time" → substrate timestamp difference. "Cadence parameter N" → record-type field. "Exceeds" → numerical comparison.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass.** `promotion` glossary-canonical. `demotion-candidacy` reduces to `demotion` (glossary) + outcome-state.

**Overall: Pass.** (The cleanest substantive cell across §2.5.)

##### B6 — AND-composition

- **1.1 Violation — Pass with caveat.** Falsifying state at structural level: a hypothesis's candidacy marker is set true while either `LayerA_outcome` or `LayerB_outcome` is false. Caveat: Layer B's semantic content is deferred (B(Layer-B-deferred)); the structural test on the marker-relationship is independent of Layer B's deferred shape.
- **1.2 Observation — Pass with caveat.** Third party inspects three markers (candidacy, Layer A, Layer B) and verifies AND. Mechanical at the marker layer. Caveat: what counts as "Layer B = true" is deferred.
- **1.3 Operationalization — Pass with caveat.** "AND" → logical conjunction over two booleans. "Layer A" defined in B(Layer-A). "Layer B" defined in B(Layer-B-deferred). AND-of-flags is operationalizable today; Layer B's specific structural test is deferred.
- **1.4 Non-circularity — Pass.** AND is a logical operator; layers are defined in their elements.

**Overall: Pass with caveat (Layer B semantic content deferred; structural AND is testable on flags).**

#### Forward-reference question — Layer B (deferred deep criterion)

The B(Layer-B-deferred) literal Fail on tests 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 raises a methodological question: does §4's falsifiability discipline reject the claim from §2.5 binding text (Reading 1 — Fail-by-§4), or does the [§2 L41 precedent](../constitutional-charter.md#2-constitutional-invariants) admit it as a tracked forward reference (Reading 2 — Pass-by-forward-reference)?

##### Reading 1 — Fail-by-§4

**Supporting evidence within existing project decisions:**

- [§4 frozen v0.2 Definition](../constitutional-charter.md#4-constitutional-design-rule): "A constitutional claim is admissible **if and only if** it is structurally falsifiable." The biconditional admits no exception clause.
- [§4 Forbidden Anti-Patterns](../constitutional-charter.md#4-constitutional-design-rule): "Adopting an invariant not structurally enforceable in schemas, types, or permitted operations. Fails criterion 1." Layer B today has no schemas or type representation.
- [`falsifiability-check` §2](../../../.claude/skills/epistemic/falsifiability-check/SKILL.md) failure modes: non-violable, non-observable, non-operationalizable. Layer B today qualifies as all three.
- [`CONTRIBUTING.md` §What This Project Is Not](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md): "If you find yourself writing prose that sounds important but cannot be falsified, rewrite it or do not include it."

**Risk if this reading is wrong:** §4's discipline becomes a procedural escape hatch; any non-falsifiable claim is admissible by labeling its operationalization deferred-to-future-RFC. The discipline collapses into a paper requirement; the §4 redaction's narrow-but-not-minimal verdict loses its enforcement value.

**Downstream consequences for §2.5 binding text:** the staged-combination form from §0011 cannot be encoded as-is. Two options: (i) strip Layer B (codify Layer A alone — contradicts §0011); or (ii) invert [§0008's redaction order](../decision-log.md) — redact §2.4 and §2.6 first (requires charter-amendment-style RFC for the calendar-discipline change).

**Methodological consequences:** Reading 1 forces every Charter invariant whose binding text references a pending sibling to be redaction-blocked until all dependencies are redacted. Calendar discipline becomes maximally restrictive — possibly unworkable.

##### Reading 2 — Pass-by-forward-reference

**Supporting evidence within existing project decisions:**

- **[§2 L41 (frozen per v0.1.1)](../constitutional-charter.md#2-constitutional-invariants) is the precedent.** The frozen §2 header contains: "The criteria above are themselves recorded formally in [Section 4 — Constitutional Design Rule](#4-constitutional-design-rule) (pending)." This is frozen Charter text containing an admitted forward reference to then-pending §4. The "(pending)" parenthetical marks the deferral mechanically. §4 was later redacted (v0.2); the reference became fulfilled. [`decision-log §0007`](../decision-log.md) registers the residual "(pending)" parenthetical as queued for editorial correction — acknowledging that the forward reference was structurally admitted even while target was pending.
- **[`decision-log §0011`](../decision-log.md) explicitly invokes the precedent.** The Q4 Constitutional review reads: "Layer B's structural falsifiability is deferred to its follow-on RFC, which is a **procedural deferral, not a non-falsifiable claim**. The forward-reference to §2.6 / §2.4 (pending) **follows the precedent of [Charter §2 L41]**." The Q4 committee already articulated Reading 2 as the operative framing for Layer B's admission.
- **[§0008's redaction order](../decision-log.md) PRESUMES Reading 2.** The order (§2.5 → §2.3 → §2.4 → §2.6) is dependency-from-root, not leaf-first. §0008 states: "§2.5 has the broadest dependency surface (defines Category III operations — formation, merge, split, promotion, demotion, dissolution — that §2.3, §2.4, and §2.6 all reference)." Without Reading 2, the order would necessarily have been §2.6 → §2.4 → §2.3 → §2.5.
- **[§4 Rationale's fixed-point reading](../constitutional-charter.md#4-constitutional-design-rule) is structurally analogous.** §4 records: "The recursion is not vicious: the test procedure is defined externally to §4 … and §4's claims reduce to procedural artifacts. The chain bottoms out in procedure, not in self-reference." Reading 2 adopts the same chain-bottoms-out-in-procedure structure: forward references reduce to calendar-discipline procedural artifacts (§0008 + the follow-on RFC), and substantive falsifiability is enforced at per-sibling redaction time, not at the forward-reference admission.

**Risk if this reading is wrong:** §0011's framing is wrong; §0008's calendar discipline rests on a misreading; §2 L41 contains a non-falsifiable forward reference whose admission was procedurally erroneous. Every Charter forward reference becomes a latent §4-falsifiability failure that re-opens for re-litigation each time the referenced section's status shifts.

**Downstream consequences for §2.5 binding text:** the binding text codifies the staged-combination structure with Layer B as a forward reference to §2.6 / §2.4 (pending). The reference uses the §2 L41 pattern (section references with `(pending)` parentheticals; the Layer B follow-on RFC cited as tracking mechanism). The forward reference creates an unfilled-but-tracked structural obligation, resolved at per-sibling redaction time.

**Methodological consequences:** Reading 2 generalizes to a Charter-wide forward-reference admission. §2.3 may forward-reference §2.4; §2.4 may forward-reference §2.6. The chain bottoms out at §2.6 (whose Ontology Open Question 3 dependency is resolved via §2.6's own pre-Gate). Forward references admissible when (a) the target is a pending invariant scheduled in the calendar discipline, and (b) the reference uses the §2 L41 pattern (explicit section number + `(pending)` marker). The discipline scales to §3 Non-Goals.

##### Recommendation

**Reading 2 — Pass-by-forward-reference is recommended.**

Grounded in four convergent considerations:

1. [§0011](../decision-log.md) already invokes Reading 2 explicitly as the operative framing for Layer B's admission. Step 1.2 is not the first place this question is litigated.
2. [§2 L41](../constitutional-charter.md#2-constitutional-invariants) establishes the structural pattern in frozen Charter text; the admission has not been overturned.
3. [§0008's calendar discipline](../decision-log.md) PRESUMES Reading 2 in its dependency ordering.
4. [§4 Rationale's fixed-point reading](../constitutional-charter.md#4-constitutional-design-rule) is structurally analogous; methodological precedent is internal-consistency.

##### What would reverse this recommendation

- A finding that §4's "if and only if" admits no exception and that the §2 L41 admission was procedurally erroneous. Requires overturning §0007's editorial-correction framing AND §0011's procedural-deferral framing.
- A finding that the §2 L41 case is not analogous: §4 was meta-prose; Layer B is substrate-binding. Under this asymmetry, Reading 2 may admit meta forward references but not object-level ones. The committee at Step 1.5 would need to articulate the meta/object asymmetry and decide whether it severs the precedent.
- A pragmatic finding during Step 1.5 binding-text drafting that the §2 L41 pattern cannot be written for Layer B without inducing a §4-fail in the wording itself.

##### Required for binding text under Reading 2

The §2.5 binding text MUST do all of the following:

1. **Name the forward-referenced invariants explicitly.** Inline references to §2.6 and §2.4 by section number with current status (pending). The reference is opaque-on-shape but transparent-on-target; §2 L41 is the structural template.
2. **Cite the Layer B follow-on RFC by name.** [`ontology-revision-layer-b-deep-criterion`](../../rfcs/draft/ontology-revision-layer-b-deep-criterion.md) (opened per §0011) is the canonical tracking artifact for the deferred specification.
3. **Use a structural marker mirroring §2 L41's `(pending)` parenthetical pattern.** The marker makes the deferral mechanically detectable.
4. **Document the falsifiability obligation that becomes binding at sibling redaction.** The binding text states explicitly that Layer B's structural shape becomes operationalized when §2.6 and §2.4 are redacted; the deferred obligation is recorded in the binding text itself.
5. **Preserve the AND composition as the structural form asserted today.** Layer A AND Layer B is the structural form; AND is operationalizable today on the marker relationship; only Layer B's specific test shape is deferred.
6. **Reference §0011's recorded AND-composition fragility.** The five reversal conditions enumerated in [`q4-evidence.md` Phase 5](../../rfcs/discussion/q4-evidence.md) are not enumerated in §2.5 binding text; §2.5 cites §0011 as the canonical record of the trade-off.
